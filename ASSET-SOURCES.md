# Artwork and media sources

The site uses GeruDoku block and item textures and the gameplay trailer/screenshots supplied for the Shadow of Israphel fan project.

The companion artwork is a 3D render of the skins selected for the game: the owner-selected Honeydew dwarf skin and a preserved Xephos red-uniform candidate uploaded by 20super in 2014. These match the early character designs; the exact original 2011 PNG revisions have not been established. Skin pixels were not repainted. Tools were modeled from the project item textures and posed at the hands.

The scene uses Three.js to produce a static transparent PNG; the public site does not load a 3D rendering library. The screenshot gallery contains actual gameplay images.

Map, skin, texture, character, and other third-party rights remain with their respective creators. No new rights clearance is implied.

The November 2026 website refinement reuses these existing assets. `assets/media/previews/` contains resized, compressed copies of the six approved 1920×1080 gameplay captures (640px gallery thumbnails and 1280px previews). The originals, approved MP4, companion render, textures, and local font are unchanged. No new artwork or gameplay imagery was generated. Historical reference screenshots are review evidence outside the deployable source, not website graphics.
