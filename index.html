REMEMBER WHERE IT ALL BEGAN?

Some adventures stay with you.

The first shelter. The familiar faces. The feeling that something much bigger was waiting just over the next hill. Shadow of Israphel was more than a series to grow up with. It was a world you wanted to be part of.

This independent fan project is rebuilding that world as a playable Minecraft adventure. Familiar places, the original story, and Honeydew and Xephos by your side. One block at a time.

A NOTE FROM THE DEVELOPER

Built with a lot of heart.

I'm a solo developer, serving full-time in the U.S. Army and raising a family. In the spare hours in between, I'm rebuilding a little piece of the world I grew up with.

This is a passion project, not a business. It'll take time, and I won't promise a date I can't confidently deliver. Thanks for being here while it takes shape.

Before you set off

A few things you might be wondering.

Is this an official project?

No. This is an independent fan recreation, not affiliated with or endorsed by The Yogscast, Mojang Studios, or Microsoft.

Can I play it yet?

Not yet. The project is in private development. There is no public download or announced release date.

Will it be free?

The intention is to release it for free. I'm not accepting donations. Any eventual release will depend on readiness and the appropriate permissions.

Where can I see gameplay?

Watch the development trailer at the top of this page, or explore the screenshot gallery. Everything shown is from the adventure in development.

What kind of adventure is it?

A playable recreation of the Shadow of Israphel world and story in Minecraft. The main quest comes first, with optional exploration and side activities planned along the way.
