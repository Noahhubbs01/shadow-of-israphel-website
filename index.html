Where it stands

1 NOVEMBER 2026PRIVATE BUILD

The opening and Episodes 1–2 are playable, followed by exploration around Mistral. Companions, story scenes, conversations, optional errands, and shops are working in the current build.

The later episodes still need to be built. There’s no public download or release date yet.

About the project

I’m making this in my spare time around full-time Army service and family life. Shadow of Israphel was a big part of growing up for me, and I wanted to revisit it as something you could play.

I intend to release it for free when it’s ready and the asset permissions are in place. I’m not accepting donations.
