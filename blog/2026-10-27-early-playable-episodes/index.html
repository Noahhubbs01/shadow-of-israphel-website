27 October 2026 · retrospective note

Joining the first two episodes together

The opening through Episodes 1–2 can be played in one private build, with conversations and companion actions along the way.

Looking back at work recorded on 27 October 2026. Written 3 November 2026.

The opening and Episodes 1–2 now connect in the private playtest build. You can continue into the second episode without starting the opening again or resetting your inventory.

This pass worked on conversation choices, companion actions, the bridge, and the route into Mistral. Choices can be made with the mouse or keyboard, and conversations can wait until you’re ready.

The development checks reached both episode endings, but that doesn’t make them finished. Pacing, staging, and the match to the original series still need review. The checks also used development shortcuts, so they aren’t a substitute for playing through normally.
