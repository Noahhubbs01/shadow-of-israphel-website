The first gameplay trailer

A first look at the private build, from the Yogcave to the airships above Mistral.

Looking back at work recorded on 1 November 2026. Written 3 November 2026.

The first gameplay trailer is up. It’s a short look around the private build: familiar places, Honeydew and Xephos, and the journey into Mistral.

The opening and Episodes 1–2 are playable, with some room to explore Mistral afterwards. Conversations, companions, errands, and shops are in the build. There’s still testing to do, and the later episodes haven’t been built yet.

This is a look at development, rather than a release announcement. There’s no public download or date to promise. I’ll keep sharing progress as the work moves along.

You can watch on YouTube or join the existing Reddit discussion.
