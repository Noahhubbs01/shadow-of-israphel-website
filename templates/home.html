One person, spare evenings.

Shadow of Israphel was a big part of growing up for me. I wanted to revisit it as something you could play.

I’m the only developer. I work full-time in the U.S. Army and have a family, so this moves along in my spare time.

Where it stands

The opening and Episodes 1–2 are playable in the private build, with companions, conversations, errands, and shops around Mistral. Episodes 3–4 have now passed complete Minecraft test runs in the development build; personal playtesting and corrections are still to come.

Work is continuing through Episodes 5–7. The original story remains the foundation, with extra exploration and activities built around it.

A passion project

This is independent, unofficial, and noncommercial. I intend to release it for free when it’s ready and the asset permissions are in place. There’s no public download or promised release date, and I’m not accepting donations.
