Community suggestions

Small details matter when you remember this story by heart. This is where I’m keeping track of community feedback that’s making its way into the adventure, with credit to the people who pointed it out.

The changes below are implemented and tested in the private development build. Further playtesting is ongoing; there’s no public download yet.

From feedback to the game

Implemented in private build · 13 November 2026

Let the loss of the Yogcave hurt

Suggested by @BobbyTheWallflower on YouTube

The reaction to the ruined Yogcave needed more of the emotion remembered from the series.

Honeydew now visibly cries, and Xephos shows his own distress before comforting him. After Honeydew’s first shout, all four companions gather on the surviving wooden platform for the grief scene. Their approach is shorter, the outbursts use full capitals, and the scene takes its time before easing back into the boots and insurance jokes.

Implemented in private build · 13 November 2026

Give Peculier’s family home its weight

Suggested by @BobbyTheWallflower on YouTube

Peculier’s reaction to his own ruined home also needed a little more grief.

His outcry is stronger, his voice breaks in the dialogue, and he bows his head at the family home. He remains visibly shaken through the attempt to put out the fire, until the player helps him leave. The existing story and uncertainty around the portrait remain intact.
