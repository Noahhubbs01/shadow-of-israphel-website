The private build now has story progression implemented through **Episode 7**. Complete Minecraft test runs have passed for Episodes 1–7, and I've started putting it through a longer personal playtest. That is progress, but it is still a work in progress — not a finished release.

I've put together a new **18:39 gameplay showcase with no commentary**, from the portal and the ruined Yogcave to Mistral and Fumblemore's tower. This video stops at the ingredient quest; it does **not** show all seven episodes. It keeps the opening cinematic, uses music from the series soundtrack, and plays here in **1080p at 60 FPS**.

**Watch directly on the site:** the full video is below, or [watch it at the top of the homepage](/#playthrough). You can also [watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/x04XBUYBdSk). The [original gameplay trailer](/#trailer) is still available too.

The playtest highlighted more work for the next pass, particularly companion movement, portal staging, scene timing, music behaviour and smaller quest/dialogue details. Optional quests and free roaming between main-story scenes have already received fixes, while the rest of that feedback is logged for the next stage.

I'd really appreciate honest, constructive criticism. What feels like Shadow of Israphel, and what breaks the feeling? Comments on the pacing, quest flow, dialogue, visuals or NPC behaviour are all useful. You can [join the project discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/Yogscast/comments/1xlg74g/shadow_of_israphel_fan_adventure_first_gameplay/). Timestamps help me track down anything that looks wrong.

The screenshots below are real captures from this November playtest: the high bridge to Fumblemore's tower, Mistral's fountains, and Ms Miggles reunited with Sir Marmalade. They show the earlier part of the adventure featured here, not Episode 7 footage.

There is still no public download or promised release date. This remains an independent, unofficial, noncommercial fan project.
