The first community critique was a good one: the return to the Yogcave needed more of the emotion people remember from the series. **@BobbyTheWallflower on YouTube** also pointed out Peculier’s reaction to his own ruined home. Thank you for calling both out.

I’ve finished a hotpatch for those two scenes in the **private development build**. After Honeydew’s first shout about their home, all four companions now reach the surviving wooden platform before the grief and comfort begin. I shortened their approach so the scene gets there sooner. Honeydew visibly cries, Xephos is upset too, and then he moves into comforting Honeydew. Their emotional outbursts use full capitals. The inspection, boots, and insurance jokes still follow, with room for the loss to sink in first.

Peculier now has a stronger outcry at his family home, a breaking voice in the dialogue, and a bowed, grieving posture. He stays visibly shaken through the attempt to put out the fire, until the player helps him leave. I checked the original homecoming scenes while making these changes; the added performance cues support the existing story without inventing answers about the portrait or the destruction.

The game checks covered the Yogcave platform arrivals, the grief and comfort poses, and saving and reloading midway through the scene without repeating the emotional dialogue. A focused Peculier scene check also confirmed his grief pose and its release after the player’s choice to leave. The current automated checks pass: 238 script tests and 66 Java tests. These are targeted checks; broader playtesting is still ongoing.

I’ve also opened a [community suggestions page](/community/) to keep feedback, contributor credits, and the resulting changes together. Both suggestions are recorded there. This update is part of the private build’s development, with no public download yet.
