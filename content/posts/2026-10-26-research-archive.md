There’s a lot to keep straight when revisiting Shadow of Israphel: where people stood, when a conversation happened, and how a place changed between visits.

The research archive has an index and a first pass through all 42 episodes, with scene notes, dialogue candidates, and references to the original material. Historical map versions are kept separately so they can be compared without changing the originals.

A first pass is a starting point. It doesn’t mean every line or movement has been verified, or that all 42 episodes have been built. The uncertain details are recorded for later checking. I want the original story to remain the basis of the adventure while being honest about the parts that still need work.
